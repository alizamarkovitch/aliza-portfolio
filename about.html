About

I'm a Senior Product Designer with 7+ years turning ambiguous, rules-heavy problems into clear direction — across banking, healthcare, and large-scale marketplaces. Research is where that direction actually comes from: I'm at my best when I can get close to how people really work — through field research, usability testing, or just watching where a workaround has quietly become the process — and use that to build the case for a better mental model, then negotiate the trade-offs across product, engineering, and leadership to get there.

At Royal Bank of Canada, I ramped independently across three teams in 18 months and led the design of bringing joint account opening online for the first time — previously, it could only be done in branch. That meant reframing the entire mental model of the acquisition flow: from one person opening one sole account, to introducing ownership type — sole or joint — into the flow itself. That work landed at the same time the bank was introducing multi-product account opening, so the flow had to account for both dimensions of complexity at once: it went from one person opening one account to multiple people opening multiple accounts. Research grounded the direction throughout — validating the ownership model against an existing, proven pattern already used in online banking, rather than introducing something untested. I explored three competing UX architectures for the combined problem, presented the trade-offs to leadership, and helped align product, engineering, legal, and platform teams through two major pivots as the surrounding flows changed underneath it.

At League, I led design of the Customer Support Portal, replacing a workaround where support agents logged directly into member accounts. Discovery research with agents surfaced what they actually needed versus what they'd improvised around a tool never built for them — closing a real privacy risk with a dedicated, secure interface for 30+ client teams. I also led League Console, an end-to-end configuration platform that removed engineering as a bottleneck for non-technical business users; usability testing across 9 users and 5 internal teams shaped the navigation model and reduced admin setup time by 40%.

At Accenture Song, I designed end-to-end iOS and web experiences for brands including Tim Hortons and TD Bank, translating business objectives into user journeys through research and iterative testing. Before that, at Delivery Hero, I led UX research and redesign of Go Delivery — including field research across 12 locations in Malaysia — for an order-management platform used by 500K+ restaurants worldwide, shipping a swimlane-based system preferred by 85% of users in field testing.

When I'm not refining user flows or iterating on prototypes, you'll likely find me immersed in horticulture and floral design. Spending time in community gardens and creating bouquets keeps me grounded and reminds me of the patience and empathy needed to nurture great ideas. This hands-on passion for growth directly informs my approach to design: a belief that every product needs consistent care, attention, and a little creativity to truly flourish.
